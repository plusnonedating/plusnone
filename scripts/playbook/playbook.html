Welcome

You signed up.
Here's what next.

Plus None turns your business or event into a dating pool — a geo-gated, opt-in space where the singles in your room can find each other. The software is the easy part. We've already activated your geofence. Your digital brand kit is in your inbox, ready to download, print, and place.

The rest is on you.

Save it. Share it with your team. Refer back to section 06 when your first report lands.

02 · First 48 hours04

First 48 hours

Two days of prep beats
two months of guessing.

A quick checklist for the two days after you sign up.

Day 0 — Right now

Your geofence is live within 48 hours of payment. You'll get a confirmation email when it activates, plus a link to download your Plus None brand kit — QR signage in multiple formats, table tents, mirror clings, and social templates. If you don't see it within two business days, email plusnone@fetewell.com.

Day 1 — Brief your team

Send section 05 of this playbook to everyone working the floor — bartenders, servers, hosts, door staff. They'll get asked "what's that?" within the first shift. They need the 30-second answer ready.

Day 2 — Sketch your placement plan

Walk your space twice — once at peak hours, once at slow hours. Mark the spots where a single person would be standing still for at least five seconds. Section 03 or 04 tells you which spots win and which lose.

Day 3 — Print and place

Send the kit to any print shop — Staples, Office Depot, VistaPrint, your local print and copy — or run it off a decent office printer for day one. Place it according to your plan. Take a photo of each placement and text it to your manager — that's your audit trail when staff move things around.

Two days of prep beats two months of guessing.
03 · Signage · Business05

Placement

Bathroom mirror beats
every other location.

Captive audience. Good lighting. Five seconds of waiting. They scan because there's nothing else to do.

Priority order

  1. Bathroom mirrors (both)
  2. Bar top, by the register
  3. Table tents on two-tops
  4. Coat check / entry counter
  5. Patio standing tables

Bathroom mirrors. Highest-converting spot. One cling in every bathroom.

Bar top, by the register. Where solo patrons sit. Eye-level when waiting for a drink.

Table tents on two-tops. Skip four-tops — those are date nights or friend groups.

Coat check / entry counter. First and last impression.

Patio standing tables. Singles wander out alone. Catch them.

Don't

  • Hide it behind a bottle display or menu board.
  • Put it in the kitchen pass or back-of-house.
  • Tape it to a window where backlight kills the QR.
  • Move it after the first week. Let it become part of the room.
A QR code in the wrong spot is wallpaper. In the right spot, it's a doorway.
04 · Signage · Events06

Placement

Restrooms first.
Then registration.

For festivals, conferences, conventions, and faires running Plus None as a one-time activation.

Priority order

  1. Restroom mirrors and stall doors
  2. Registration and badge pickup
  3. Food and drink areas
  4. Lounge and chill zones
  5. Smoking and vape areas

Restroom mirrors and stall doors. Same logic as bars. Number-one performer at every event.

Registration and badge pickup. Day-one moment. Attendees alone, waiting in line.

Food and drink areas. Coffee carts, bars, snack stations. Singles congregate.

Lounge and chill zones. Couches, charging stations, quiet rooms. Singles seek these out.

Don't

  • Put signage in vendor-only or staff-only areas.
  • Compete with stage signage, sponsor signage, or wayfinding.
  • Put it on a column where it gets covered the next day.
The hallway is the program. Treat it like one.
05 · Training your team07

The 30-second script

If your team can't answer
in 30 seconds, the scan
doesn't happen.

When a patron asks "what's this for?", here's what to say:

"It's Plus None — scan and see who else here is single right now. Just for our venue, anonymous outside it. People meet through it. Try it."

What not to say

  • Don't call it a "dating app." It's geo-gated, opt-in, anonymous outside the venue.
  • Don't pressure. If they ask once and don't scan, drop it.
  • Don't gatekeep. Anyone can scan.

Common questions

"Who can see my profile?"
Only other people physically inside this venue who also opted in. Nothing persists outside.

"What does it cost?"
Nothing. Free to opt in.

"How do I get my info off?"
Tap "remove me" in the feed. Or wait — your session auto-ends when the timer runs out.

06 · Reading your report08

Monthly report

Five numbers that
actually matter.

First of the month. By email. Subject: "Your Plus None report — [Month] [Year]."

Scans. How many people opted into your dating pool last month. Anything is a win in month one.

Return rate (30-day). How many came back and scanned again within 30 days. Watch it month over month — when it climbs, you're building regulars.

Peak night. The night with the most scans. Staff smarter — your strongest server on that night.

Demographic mix. Age and gender breakdown. Often surprises owners.

Social pull. Submissions that converted to a follow of @plusnonedating and tagged your venue.

What to do with it

  • Staff your peak night with your strongest team.
  • Run a promotion on your slowest night — target the demographic you're already pulling.
  • Quote the numbers to your landlord or investors. "Our scans grew 40% MoM" closes a refinance.
The businesses that read it benefit more than the ones that don't.

08 · Troubleshooting10

When things go wrong

The handful of things
that go wrong.

Most of it is one email away.

Lost your brand kit email. Email plusnone@fetewell.com with the business name on the account — we'll resend the download link.

The QR code isn't scanning. Three usual culprits: direct sunlight or reflective surface (move it), grease or sticker covering the code (wipe or replace), bad camera moment (refresh the camera app).

You need the signage reprinted. Everything in the brand kit is in print-ready vector and high-res PNG. Send it to any print shop or run it off your office printer. Email us if you want format advice or a vendor recommendation.

A patron complains about privacy. Nothing persists outside their session. The venue can't see their profile. They can remove themselves anytime. For deletion requests, email plusnone@fetewell.com.

You want to cancel. Email plusnone@fetewell.com from the account's billing email. Geofence deactivates at the end of your billing cycle; no further charges.

You want to add a second location. Email plusnone@fetewell.com. Multi-location pricing exists.
